# Support Policy

## Severity

- `urgent`: Production is blocked, money movement is affected, or a deadline is within the same business day.
- `high`: A core workflow is broken, but there is a workaround or no immediate revenue impact.
- `normal`: The customer needs guidance, account help, or a non-blocking fix.

## Routing

- Billing questions go to the Billing queue.
- Sign-in and account access issues go to the Account queue.
- Production API failures go to the Engineering Escalation queue.
- Product usage questions go to the Support queue.

## Reply Style

- Acknowledge the specific issue.
- State the immediate next step.
- Ask for only the minimum missing information.
- Do not promise refunds, credits, or engineering timelines.
